How The Non-AI Pipeline Works

1. Kuwahara Brushstrokes

Divides pixels into 4 directional lobes and gathers the sector with the lowest color variance. Removes digital noise while turning flat video into hand-painted artistic brush strokes.

2. Capillary Bleed & Warm Blooms

Simulates wet-in-wet watercolor bleeding. Deep reds, sunny cadmiums, and golden yellows seep into neighboring washes with organic capillary gradients.

3. Cold-Press Paper & Cream Highlights

Procedural cold-press watercolor paper grain pools pigment into surface troughs. Bright highlights roll off gently into creamy parchment tones rather than digital white.
